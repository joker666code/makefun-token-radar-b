Three lenses: Implied value ranks by the model above; Early movers ranks by momentum × youth, the protocols moving fastest while still young; Value density ranks by implied value per dollar of TVL, the protocols whose TVL converts most efficiently. The board covers all 40 watchlist protocols plus 158 more below the $50M floor.

Why the board totals $4.99B — 3.7× the $1.35B a flat 0.08× multiple gives on the same snapshot: this board's multiples come from each category's own token-having peers (the snapshot's global median is 0.214×, not 0.08×), and the biggest categories carry above-median multiples — Risk Curators, which alone holds 54.9% of the board's implied value, is 0.326×. The flat route's own check below its $50M floor found a median of 0.419× among 76 smaller token-having protocols, so a single 0.08× understates small protocols rather than flattering them; the category multiples are the same evidence, applied per category.

The five factors and the 34 category multiples — the whole model
  • Category multiple. The median market-cap / TVL of the token-having protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the snapshot's global median when a category has few peers, then clamped to 0.05–0.80. No name is ever hard-coded: the multiples come from this snapshot's own figures.
  • Momentum. 1 + 0.4 × the 7-day TVL change, winsorised to −40%…+60%. A spike above +60% is capped and flagged — check it is not mercenary capital before you trust it.
  • Seasoning. 0.75 + 0.25 × age / 3 years. Older protocols with no token yet have survived longer without one; unknown age takes the 0.75 floor.
  • Breadth. +2% per extra chain, at most +8%.
  • Credibility. Starts at 1.00 and loses 0.03 for a missing 7-day change, 0.03 for a missing listing date, 0.02 for TVL under $10M, 0.02 for a single chain (floor 0.90). Thin or incomplete records are penalised, never rewarded — and nobody is dropped from the board for them.
